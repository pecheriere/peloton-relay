Meet Pacer
In running, the rabbit sets the pace. Pacer is a small hare in a headband, tank top and white sneakers, and shows how the relay is doing: the tank top takes the status color.
Flat shapes, no outlines
Works from 24 px to 240 px
Small sizes use the head only: ears, headband, face.
Tank top = state. Accent when ready, then the zone color of the status. Ears carry the mood.
Not a logo. No Peloton marks, red or wordmark: the relay is unofficial.
