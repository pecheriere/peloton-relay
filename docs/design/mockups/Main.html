Relay
Your Peloton data, ready for your agents. An unofficial, self-hosted relay.
design language v0.1
Light and dark · iPhone first · any modern browser
Principle 1
Calm until it matters
Neutral ground, one accent. Color appears only to say something: a status, a zone, the one action to take.
Principle 2
Zones are the palette
The seven training-zone colors carry data and status everywhere, so every chart and badge feels like the ride.
Principle 3
One tap, one thing
Every screen has a single primary action within thumb reach. Risky actions ask once, plainly, and say what happens.
